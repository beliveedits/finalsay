Finalsay local AI

Experimental. Not verified on a live setup yet.

This page asks a model running on your own computer to suggest a next move for a lead. You read the suggestion. If you click "Queue for approval", it goes to the Approvals tab of your Sheet and waits there. Nothing is applied from this page, and nothing is ever sent to a client.

Small local models make things up. They may say "I have emailed the client" when they did nothing. Finalsay ignores such claims. It only reads four fields from the model's answer: a stage, a next step, a number of days and a draft reply.
